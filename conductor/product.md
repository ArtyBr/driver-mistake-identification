# Product Definition

## Initial Concept
B, E - learner drivers to prepare and know if they are making common but hard-to-spot (for non-trained instructors) mistakes.

## Target Audience
- **Individual Drivers:** Specifically learner drivers who want to review their driving sessions to identify mistakes.
- **Driving Instructors:** Professionals who can use the system as an auxiliary tool to show students concrete examples of their driving errors (e.g., failing to check mirrors before merging).

## Core Value Proposition
- **Automated "Shadow" Instructor:** Acts as a vigilant, always-on safety monitor that catches subtle errors a human passenger might miss or forget to mention.
- **Objective Feedback:** Provides data-driven insights (e.g., "You failed to check your left mirror 3 times before merging") rather than subjective criticism, helping learners accept and correct their behavior.
- **Enhanced Safety Awareness:** By correlating head pose with road events, it teaches the crucial habit of situational awareness—looking *before* acting—which is the foundation of defensive driving.

## Key Features
- **Dual-View Synchronization:** Automatically syncs road and driver video feeds to provide a complete context of every driving event.
- **Automated Mistake Detection:** Uses computer vision to identify specific unsafe behaviors, such as lane deviations without mirror checks or merging while distracted.
- **Interactive Review Dashboard:** A web-based interface where users can upload their drive videos and view a timeline of flagged "safety events," clicking to jump to the exact moment of the mistake.
- **Visual Evidence:** Overlays lane detection lines and gaze classification (e.g., "Looking at Radio") on the video playback, making the cause of the error visually undeniable.
- **Session Reports:** Generates a summary for each uploaded drive, scoring the session based on the number and severity of mistakes found.
